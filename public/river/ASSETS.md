# Photographic scene assets

Provider: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/). License: [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

- [Kloofendal Overcast PureSky](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_overcast_puresky): 4K HDR sky.
- [Monbachtal Riverbank](https://polyhaven.com/a/monbachtal_riverbank): 4K HDR forest lighting.
- [Rock Moss Set 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01): six scanned rock variants and 2K PBR textures.
- [Pine Sapling Small](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_small): three complete tree variants and 2K PBR textures.
- [Pine Tree 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01): branch atlas and bark material retained for scene development.
- [Grass Bermuda 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_bermuda_01): 21 scanned grass variants and 2K PBR textures.
- [Forest Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor): 2K color and OpenGL normal maps.

`assets.json` records source download URLs, source checksums and local verification. All scene files are served locally, with no runtime dependency on external hosts. Photographic materials are applied to three-dimensional geometry; water is rendered and simulated in real time.
